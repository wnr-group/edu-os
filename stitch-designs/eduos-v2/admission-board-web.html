One pipeline, two front doors. Cards arrive from the public /apply form or an Add enquiry walk-in — identical from here on. Optional steps (entrance test, interview, document check) live on the card, not as columns, so a school that just does “enquiry → offer → enrol” isn’t dragging through empty stages. Unpaid applications stay off the board until payment clears (see the Awaiting payment filter). Moving a card to Enrolled opens the enrol dialog — nothing becomes a student by accident.
